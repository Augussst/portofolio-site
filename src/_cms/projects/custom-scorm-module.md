* Custom Framework Architecture: Developed a proprietary e-learning authoring workspace utilizing Astro and Alpine.js to generate lightweight, highly interactive, and responsive SCORM packages outside of traditional authoring tools.
* Agentic Authoring Workflow: Integrated AI Coding Assistants (Agentic AI) within VS Code to automate the heavy lifting of instructional design—from initial module planning and scriptwriting to generating the technical slide-by-slide storyboard.
* Human-in-the-Loop Quality Control: Engineered a strict, multi-stage validation pipeline. AI agents cannot proceed to the next phase (Planning > Storyboarding > Development) without explicit human expert approval, ensuring 100% accuracy and compliance with internal company materials.
* Component Showcase: *Note: The core repository is private to protect corporate IP. The live demo provided showcases the frontend interactive components and navigational flow generated by this custom engine.*
